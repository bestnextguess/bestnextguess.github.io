How the odds work: the percentages are estimates from answer history. Since Feb 2, 2026, about 1 in 10 daily answers has been a repeat, so never-used words share about 89% of the odds and repeats about 11%, split again among whichever words still fit. For an older puzzle, the answer must already have been used, so only repeats count. The next move is the word that should finish the puzzle in the fewest guesses: it plans ahead, works out small groups of words exactly, and keeps track of how many guesses you have left.
