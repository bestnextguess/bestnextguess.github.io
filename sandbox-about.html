About Best Next Guess

A free helper for the daily Wordle. Type in your guesses and colors, and it shows every word that still fits, the odds for each one, and your smartest next move.

Free. No ads. Always.

No ads, no pop-ups, no sign-up and nothing to install. There's no trial and no paid version. It's a hobby project made for people who love the game, and it will stay free.

How it works

The odds. Wordle now sometimes reuses old answers, but only about 1 in 10 answers since February 2, 2026 has been a repeat. So the site estimates that words never used as the answer share about 89% of the odds and past answers about 11%, split again among whichever words still fit. Less common words get a slice of the never-used odds that follows the last 60 days of answers, and common words get a little more weight than rare ones, because that is how the answers have actually been chosen. The list of past answers updates itself every day.

The next move. The engine picks the guess that should finish the puzzle in the fewest guesses. It plans ahead, works out small groups of words exactly, and keeps track of how many guesses you have left. In hard mode, that steers you around the word families that break streaks.

Your choice. When a different word has a real chance of being the answer right now, it shows that too, as the best shot to win now.

After your game. Already played? Use a screenshot of your finished board, or type it in, and you get a game report: each guess next to what the helper would have played, how many words were left, and where luck came in. Step through it guess by guess, the way you'd replay a chess game.

Past answers. Wondering whether a word has already been a Wordle answer? Check any word on Has it been a Wordle answer? It updates every day and never shows today's answer, and if you're playing today's Wordle, it takes you straight to your best next guess.

Your first guess

RAISE

The site suggests RAISE, which leaves about 62 possible answers on average. Any strong opener works; the game is won on the guesses after it. Curious how yours does? See how it fares against every past answer.

How well it plays

Every change to the engine is tested before it goes live by replaying the past year of Wordle answers (366 puzzles), opening with RAISE and following every suggestion, using only what was known on each day:

Every game wonin normal mode, about 3.2 guesses on average
Every game wonin hard mode, about 3.2 guesses on average

On the standard research test (the original 2,315 answers, every answer equally likely, opening with SALET), it averages 3.43 guesses; perfect play averages 3.42.

The engine runs right in your browser, on your own phone or computer, so suggestions appear within a second or so.

Put it on your home screen

Best Next Guess works like an app. Add it to your home screen and it opens full-screen with its own icon, even without internet.

  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  • Android (Chrome): tap Install app at the top of the main page, or open the menu (⋮) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. In Samsung Internet: menu (≡), Add page to, Home screen.
  • Computer (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

No app store, no download and nothing to update: it always runs the latest version.

Who made it

Best Next Guess was made by Ayman Mikhail, a daily Wordle player who isn't a programmer. It started as a spreadsheet that filtered the word list by greens and yellows, and grew into this site with help from Claude, an AI assistant.

Questions, ideas or a bug to report? Email hello@bestnextguess.com. Every message gets read.

Your privacy

  • Your guesses stay in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  • A screenshot you use is read on your own device and is never uploaded.
  • Visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter: no cookies and no personal data.
  • Your device keeps a count of the days you've visited, so we can tell how many players come back. Only that count is sent, never who you are.
  • No account, no email, no tracking across other sites.

An independent fan project

Best Next Guess is not affiliated with or endorsed by The New York Times, which publishes Wordle. It doesn't know the day's answer: it works only from the colors you enter.
